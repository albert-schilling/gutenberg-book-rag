The book is a collection of short detective stories presented as a series of episodes narrated by Dr. Watson. Each episode is a self‑contained case that Holmes and Watson investigate, but the stories are linked by recurring characters and a loose chronology that moves from early adventures to later, more personal entanglements. The first story, “A Scandal in Bohemia,” introduces Holmes’s reputation, his cold rationality, and his admiration for the clever Irene Adler. Subsequent excerpts show Holmes confronting a deceptive actress, planning a night‑time raid, interrogating a suspicious young lady, and confronting secret societies such as the Ku‑Klux Klan. The narratives also feature a range of secondary figures—colonels, American maids, a jealous husband, a murderous thief, and a mysterious Mrs. Rucastle—who draw Holmes into varied settings from London streets to ruined country houses. Holmes’s method is consistently shown: careful observation, logical deduction, and occasional daring physical action. The collection builds toward a final resolution in which Holmes clears up the mystery of a sinister house, restores order for Mrs. Toller, and arranges the safe return of Miss Violet Hunter.
